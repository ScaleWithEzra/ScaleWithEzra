Lead UX Designer at [Bridger.social](https://bridger.social). B.S. UX Design at WGU, finishing 2026.

## How I work

Design decisions get made in the browser, not just in Figma. Most of what's public here is hand-built HTML and CSS: UI specimens, client microsites, and conversion funnels that shipped to real people. The heavier production work (Next.js, content pipelines, automation) lives in client repos.
